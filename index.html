SECTION 8 — ASSUMPTIONS & LIMITATIONS
Engineering scope, mathematical constraints, and disclaimers
OFFLINE RECOMMENDATION ONLY NO REAL-TIME SIGNAL CONTROL
  • Analysis uses historical traffic data ingested and validated in Step 2.
  • Webster method is used for preliminary isolated-intersection signal timing optimization.
  • Traffic demand is assumed representative of the analyzed survey period.
  • PCU factors are based on configured Step 3 engineering parameters.
  • Results are analytical/simulated estimates for decision support.
  • Queue spillback from downstream bottlenecks may not be fully represented.
  • Downstream adjacent intersection interactions are not represented in an isolated model.
  • Incident effects or temporary lane blockages are not represented.
  • Real-time stochastic traffic fluctuations are not represented.
  • Actual field implementation requires validation and sign-off by qualified traffic engineers.
