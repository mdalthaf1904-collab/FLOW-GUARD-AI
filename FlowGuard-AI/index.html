FlowGuard AI

Offline Traffic Signal Analysis, Recommendation & Simulation System

FlowGuard AI is an offline traffic signal analysis, recommendation, and simulation system. It analyzes manually entered traffic data, evaluates existing signal performance, generates candidate signal timings, compares current and proposed plans through simulation, and provides decision-support recommendations.
Note: FlowGuard AI does not directly control real-world traffic signals.

Civil Engineering Decision-Support Architecture

FlowGuard AI operates strictly as an offline decision-support prototype for traffic engineers and municipal planners. It translates approach volume counts (qi, veh/hr) into capacity metrics and evaluates bounded green-time splits to minimize overall intersection queue accumulation.

Simulated Assessment: All performance gains (e.g. queue length reduction and wait time savings) are strictly estimated improvements under simulation assumptions using deterministic D/D/1 queuing theory. Simulation results do not guarantee field performance.

⚖️ FlowGuard AI is an academic prototype intended for offline traffic analysis, simulation, and decision support. Simulation results depend on the entered data and simplified model assumptions and are not a substitute for field validation or approved traffic-signal design procedures.
