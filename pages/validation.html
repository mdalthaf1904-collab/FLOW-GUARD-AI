2. Civil Engineering D/D/1 Queue Model Audit

QUEUING THEORY SPECIFICATION
FlowGuard AI models approach queue accumulation and discharge using the classic Civil Engineering Deterministic D/D/1 Arrival-Discharge Queuing Model. Arrival rate λ and discharge rate μ are evaluated over discrete phase intervals within simulated cycles.
1. Arrival & Service Rates (veh/sec): λi = qi / 3600
μi = si / 3600 = (lanesi × sbase) / 3600
Capacity ci = si × (gi / C)  |  v/c = qi / ci
2. Queue Accumulation & Delay: Red Accumulation: Qpeak = Qprev + λi × ri
Red Delay Area: Dred = 0.5 × ri × (Qprev + Qpeak)
Oversaturation Carryover: Qend = max(0, Qpeak + λigi − μigi)

3. Implemented Engineering Assumptions vs Field Validation

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE SPECIFICATION
1. PCU & Saturation Flow
• Base saturation flow: 1800 PCU/h/lane.
• Approach saturation flow: s = Lanes × 1800.
• Standard PCU factors: Car 1.0, Bus/Truck 3.0, Auto 0.8, Motorcycle 0.5.
2. Cycle & Lost-Time Accounting
• Lost-time per phase: Lphase = Yellow (3s) + All-Red (2s) = 5s.
• Total lost time: Ltotal = Nactive × 5s.
• Total effective green: Geff = C − Ltotal.
3. Three-Stage Signal Optimizer
• Stage 1: Initial unconstrained delay optimizer.
• Stage 2: Multi-objective balanced candidate search.
• Stage 3: Deterministic simulation-validated refinement.
