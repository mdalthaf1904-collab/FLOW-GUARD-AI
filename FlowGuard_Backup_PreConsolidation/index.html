Civil Engineering Decision-Support Architecture

FlowGuard AI operates strictly as an offline decision-support prototype for traffic engineers and municipal planners. It translates raw approach volume counts (qi, veh/hr) into capacity metrics and evaluates bounded green-time splits to minimize overall intersection queue accumulation.

Simulated Assessment: All performance gains (e.g. queue length reduction and wait time savings) are strictly estimated improvements under simulation assumptions using deterministic D/D/1 queuing theory.
